About me

Engineering for the details that matter.

I’m Hariharan R, a software engineer with a background in Electronics and Communication Engineering. Since 2022, my work has focused on C++ and MFC applications for medical devices and diagnostic imaging.

At Trenser Technology Solutions, I contribute to a PACS desktop viewer for DICOM imaging studies. My work includes measurement tools, spine labeling, interactive annotations, rendering performance, and memory handling. These features bring together visual interfaces, complex application behavior, and performance optimization.

Previously, at Quest Global, I developed features for ultrasound scanner software, including StressEcho interfaces and configurable scanning protocols. I worked across requirements, UI implementation, debugging, validation, and technical documentation.

I collaborate with QA engineers, product teams, and domain experts to translate clinical requirements into dependable software. Alongside my core C++ work, I have foundational knowledge of generative AI, large language models, and prompt engineering.
